Squadgavel

A free multiplayer cricket player auction game. Create a room, share the 4-letter code with friends, pick a player pool (India all-time, Australia, England, SENA and more), and bid live on legends and current stars. When the hammer falls on the last lot, the algorithm picks each squad's best XI for Test, ODI or T20 and crowns a champion.

No accounts, no downloads: it runs peer-to-peer in your browser.
